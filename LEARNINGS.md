### How did the collaboration go?

The collaboration went well. We communicated well and always stayed in contact with each other. We divided the issues and tasks clearly. Every school day that we were present, we had a daily stand-up. We were also reachable every day when someone needed help.

### Did we achieve the goals from our team canvas?

Yes, we achieved the goals from our team canvas. However, we could have explored some topics more deeply together, so that everyone could learn more about Svelte.

### What could we improve as a team next time?

Next time, we should start working on the style guide earlier. This would help us avoid problems with fonts, colours and other design choices. It would also make the website more consistent.

We followed the code conventions from the FDND documentation and the conventions we agreed on as a team. However, we did not always follow our agreements about [commit messages](https://github.com/WebTins/your-tribe-for-life-squad-page/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md#commit-messages). Next time, we should pay more attention to this.
